SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — SERVICE AGREEMENT (for demo purposes only, not a real contract)

This Service Agreement is entered into between Acme Cloud Ltd ("Supplier") and Blue Harbour Trading LLC ("Customer").

1. Term. This Agreement is effective from the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.

2. Onboarding documents. The Customer's KYC documents must be submitted within 14 days from receipt of this Agreement.

3. Fees. The Customer shall pay each invoice within 30 days of the invoice date.

4. Termination for convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving thirty (30) days' written notice to the other party.

5. Renewal. Any request to renew on different terms must be made at least 60 days prior to the expiry of the term.

6. Data return. Upon termination, all Customer data shall be returned within 5 business days of the effective date of termination.

7. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

8. Annual report. The Supplier's annual security report must be delivered no later than 31 March 2027.
